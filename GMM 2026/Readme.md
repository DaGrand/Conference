# Privileged Access — GMM 2026

> **Why hack 200 companies when you can hack just one?**

Talk slides for the **GMM 2026** keynote on Privileged Access Management (PAM) for MSPs, presented by **David Grandolfo** (Devolutions), September 2026.

---

## About the talk

MSPs hold the keys to dozens or hundreds of client environments. For an attacker, compromising one MSP is the shortcut to all of them. This session looks at privileged access from the attacker's point of view, then gives MSP leaders a simple 30-second test and a clear definition of what an enterprise-grade PAM really is.

**Key takeaway:** PAM is what lets you answer your client, their auditor and their insurer in 30 seconds, with proof.

### The four pillars of an enterprise-grade PAM

1. **Centralized, rotated secrets**: passwords and keys centralized and changed after every use.
2. **Just-in-time access**: access granted on request, for a limited time, then revoked.
3. **Least privilege**: each account gets only the rights its task requires.
4. **Recorded sessions**: every privileged session is recorded and auditable.
